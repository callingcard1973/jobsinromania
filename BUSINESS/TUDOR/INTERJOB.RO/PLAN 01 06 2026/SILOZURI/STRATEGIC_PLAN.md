## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Low email coverage (5.8%) | Hard to reach tier 2/3 | Invest in email enrichment (Hunter, RocketReach) |
| Fragmented market (312 small operators) | Low deal size | Bundle products, focus on Tier 1 (50 large facilities) |
| Seasonal demand fluctuation | Uneven revenue | Offer annual subscriptions, develop off-season services |
| Competitor entry (established platforms) | Price pressure | Build network effects (buyer+seller matching), lock-in |
| Phone data incomplete | Outreach friction | Use LinkedIn, website research, local directories as backup |
