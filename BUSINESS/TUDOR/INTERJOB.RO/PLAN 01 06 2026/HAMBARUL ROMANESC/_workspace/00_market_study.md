# 00 — Market Study: HAMBARUL ROMANESC

**Scope:** National Romania, initial focus high-income urban areas (Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, Timișoara, Brașov, Iași). Food/grocery retail.
**Date:** 2026-06-24 · **Author:** market-research agent · **Status:** foundational (runs first)

> **Source honesty:** Hard figures are cited to INS, Euromonitor, GfK, NielsenIQ, company annual reports, or Romanian retail trade press (Retail.ro, Progresiv, ZF, Profit.ro). Where no audited figure exists, the number is labelled **(estimate)** and shown as a range. **No competitor market-share figure here is invented to a false precision** — shares are given as sourced ranges or flagged.

## 1. Market sizing — TAM / SAM / SOM

### TAM — Total Romanian grocery/FMCG retail
- Romania food & grocery retail turnover: **~€40–45 bn/year** (estimate, triangulated from INS retail-trade turnover indices + Euromonitor "Retailing in Romania" order-of-magnitude). Food/grocery is roughly half of total retail; total organized + traditional retail is well over €80 bn.
- Modern trade (chains/supermarkets/hyper/discount/proximity) ≈ **65–70%** of grocery spend (estimate; Romania's modern-trade share has climbed from ~50% a decade ago — Euromonitor/NielsenIQ trend). Traditional trade (independent shops, open markets, "la țară") ≈ 30–35%, still high vs Western EU.
- **TAM (grocery, all channels): ~€40–45 bn (estimate).**

### SAM — Serviceable Available Market
- Modern grocery trade only: **~€26–31 bn** (≈68% of TAM, estimate).
- Restrict to the 5 target metros (Bucharest + Cluj + Timișoara + Brașov + Iași) which concentrate the highest disposable income and modern-trade density. These metros hold roughly **18–22% of national population** but a **disproportionately higher grocery spend** per capita.
- **SAM (modern grocery, 5 metros): ~€6–8 bn/year (estimate).**

### SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market (HAMBARUL, realistic)
- A new differentiated chain realistically captures a **fraction of 1%** of SAM in years 1–3.
- Year 1 (2–4 pilot stores, single metro): **€4–9 M** revenue (estimate; ~€2–3 M annualized turnover per mid-size urban supermarket × pilot count, ramped).
- Year 3 (8–15 stores across 2–3 metros): **€25–45 M** (estimate).
- **SOM ≈ 0.3–0.6% of SAM by year 3 (estimate).** This is the order of magnitude to carry into the business-plan financial model — not a precise forecast.

> Cross-check anchor: an established mid-format Romanian supermarket does ~€2–4 M turnover/store/year; discounter formats far more. HAMBARUL's premium-origin positioning implies higher basket value but lower footfall than a discounter.

**Key structural facts:**
- The **discounters (Lidl, Penny) + Kaufland** dominate volume; discount is the single fastest-growing format in Romania (NielsenIQ/GfK trend).
- **Proximity (Profi, Mega Image)** has the largest store *count* footprint — Profi historically the #1 by number of stores in Romania.
- **Premium local-origin** is served today only partially: by farmers' markets, a few BIO/organic boutiques, online box schemes, and "produs românesc" shelves inside the majors — **no national pure-Romanian-producer chain exists**.

## 4. Key-player SWOT (condensed)

**Lidl / Penny (discounters)**
- S: price, scale, supply efficiency, heavy "produs românesc" advertising. W: limited assortment depth, private-label-heavy, origin is marketing-led not full-traceability. O: premiumization shelves. T: a credible origin-pure challenger exposes the gap between their PR and their actual import mix.

**Kaufland / Carrefour / Auchan (hyper)**
- S: one-stop scale, large local-supplier volumes, multi-format. W: big-box footfall declining vs proximity/discount; "local" is a corner, not the store. O: e-grocery, urban small formats. T: time-poor urban shoppers shifting to proximity + specialty.

**Mega Image / Profi (proximity)**
- S: dense urban footprint, convenience, now consolidating under Ahold Delhaize (scale). W: assortment generic; perceived as expensive convenience; origin not the core promise. O: integration synergies. T: integration disruption; differentiation-led niche entrants.

**Annabella / Diana / Unicarm (regional Romanian)**
- S: **genuine Romanian ownership + (Diana/Unicarm) own production = real origin credibility**; regional loyalty. W: regional only, limited capital, weak digital/brand at national level. O: national premium-origin brand is theirs to lose. T: a well-funded, brand-led national Romanian-producer chain could pre-empt their expansion — **these are HAMBARUL's closest reference models AND potential M&A/partnership targets.**

**La Cocoș**
- S: Romanian-owned, high-volume, disruptive value. W: value (not premium) positioning; few stores. O/T: proves Romanian-owned modern retail can scale — a positive signal, not a direct positioning competitor.

## 5. Trends

1. **Origin / "produs românesc" demand — rising.** Repeated surveys (GfK/IRSOP-type, trade press) show a majority of Romanian consumers *say* they prefer/seek Romanian products, and willingness-to-pay a premium for trusted local food is growing — but **trust the claim, not a precise %** (self-reported intent overstates behavior; mark estimate). This is the core demand tailwind.
2. **Short supply chains / farm-to-shelf.** EU CAP + national programs push short chains; consumer interest in traceability and "from the farmer" is structurally rising.
3. **BIO / organic.** Small but fast-growing base; concentrated in exactly the high-income metros HAMBARUL targets. Premium-origin and BIO overlap strongly.
4. **E-grocery.** Accelerated post-2020; Bringo/eMAG-adjacent, Freshful (eMAG), Glovo/Bolt q-commerce, plus chain own-channels. Modest share of total grocery (estimate low-to-mid single digits %) but high-growth and metro-concentrated — a viable secondary channel for HAMBARUL.
5. **Inflation behavior.** Food inflation in 2022–2024 drove trade-down to discount AND a defensive interest in trust/value-for-money — premium-origin must justify price via quality + traceability + story, not vanity premium.
6. **Discount + proximity win formats; hypermarket stagnates.** HAMBARUL should lean proximity/supermarket urban format, not big-box.

## 6. Positioning gap + opportunity sizing

### The gap
Every major (Lidl, Kaufland, Penny, Carrefour, Auchan, Mega Image, Profi) runs **"produs românesc" as a marketing layer** — a shelf, a label, a campaign — on top of an assortment that remains substantially private-label and import-mixed. Regional Romanian retailers (Annabella, Diana, Unicarm) are **origin-credible but sub-national, capital-light, and brand-weak**.

**No national chain today is a *pure* Romanian-producer retailer with full per-product traceability** (producer name, farm, county, batch) as the entire store promise. That is the white space.

### Why it's defensible
- Incumbents **cannot easily copy** a pure-origin model — it would cannibalize their efficient private-label/import economics and break their supply structure.
- Regional credible players **lack the capital/brand** to nationalize fast.
- The demand trend (origin + short-chain + BIO + traceability) is **rising and metro-concentrated** — exactly HAMBARUL's targeting.

### Opportunity sizing (estimate)
- **Addressable origin-premium niche** within the 5-metro SAM: assume **3–6%** of modern-grocery metro spend is genuinely origin-premium-seeking and willing to pay up → **~€200–480 M/year** addressable pool (estimate, = 3–6% × €6–8 bn SAM).
- HAMBARUL realistic capture by year 3 (SOM): **€25–45 M (estimate)** — i.e. low-single-digit % of the origin-premium niche, single-digit store count. Headroom is large; the constraint is execution/capital, not market size.
- **Strategic optionality:** Annabella / Diana / Unicarm are simultaneously the proof-of-concept, the benchmark, and candidate partners/acquisitions for faster national rollout.
