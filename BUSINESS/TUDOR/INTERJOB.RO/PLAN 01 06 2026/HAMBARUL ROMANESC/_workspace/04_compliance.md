# 04 - Compliance & Food Safety Checklist - HAMBARUL ROMANESC

**Pilot:** 1 grocery store, ~300 m2, Bucuresti Sector 1.
**Supplier mix (from 01_suppliers_master.csv):** dairy ~7,219 - meat/charcuterie ~1,108 - honey 1,427 - eggs 167 - cereals/grain 2,549 - bakery 1,862 - produce 858.
**Source-honest:** specific article numbers are marked "verify with ANSVSA/DSVSA/lawyer" where not certain. EU regulation names are cited as named acts. Status legend: required / in-progress / done / blocked.

> Epistemic note: Romanian implementing orders (ordine ANSVSA, ANAF) change frequently. Every numbered order below is flagged for legal verification before relying on it. Not legal advice.

## COLD-CHAIN REQUIREMENTS

Temp targets below are **general industry/EU references** - exact mandatory limits must be **confirmed against Reg. 853/2004 and RO order per product**; treat each as needing verification.

| Product group | Typical storage temp (verify) | Monitoring |
|---------------|-------------------------------|-----------|
| Fresh meat | <= +7 C carcass / <= +4 C retail - **verify per 853/2004** | Daily log, alarm on excursion |
| Minced meat / preparations | <= +2 C - **verify** | Daily log |
| Poultry | <= +4 C - **verify** | Daily log |
| Fresh dairy / pasteurized milk | <= +4 to +8 C per producer label - **follow label** | Daily log |
| Raw milk / raw-milk cheese | strict per producer + DSVSA - **verify** | Daily log + supplier rules |
| Eggs | constant temp, no fluctuation, retail typically <= +5 C - **verify** | Log |
| Frozen | <= -18 C | Daily log |

**Controls (HACCP CCPs):** calibrated thermometers per unit; min twice-daily readings logged; written corrective action on excursion (quarantine/discard); receiving-temperature check on each animal-origin delivery; document retention.

## RISK FLAGS

1. **R1 + F1 are hard blockers** - store cannot legally open or sell to public without DSVSA registration/authorization and a connected fiscal cash register. Go/no-go gates.
2. **Raw-milk & raw-milk cheese (subset of 7,219 dairy):** highest sanitary risk; special DSVSA rules + consumer warnings. Segregate for extra review before listing.
3. **Meat/charcuterie (1,108):** processing units need authorization, not just registration, plus product health mark. Verify each supplier number.
4. **Honey (1,427):** composition/labelling under Honey Directive; origin claims must be truthful; known adulteration-fraud area - keep supplier docs.
5. **BIO/ECO:** no organic certificates in master data; any bio/eco marketing non-compliant until certified. Block such labels in catalog-pricing.
6. **e-Factura / SAF-T D406 scope and start dates** depend on entity classification and shifting 2024-2025 rules; confirm with accountant; penalty risk.
7. **Allergen display for loose foods** (deli/bakery) often overlooked; ANPC/DSVSA control point.
8. **Per-supplier proof not yet collected:** dairy & meat marked blocked until DSVSA registration numbers gathered and filed (feeds R6).

## HANDOFFS
- **catalog-pricing:** enforce EU 1169/2011 mandatory label fields; no bio/eco/organic wording without certificate on file; allergen flags for dairy/bakery/meat.
- **business-plan:** budget + lead time for DSVSA authorization, HACCP consultant, hygiene training, fiscal cash register, ABP/DDD contracts; flag R1 & F1 as opening blockers.
- **supplier-sourcing:** require DSVSA registration/authorization number from every animal-origin supplier (dairy, meat, eggs, honey) before listing.
