## 2. Non-local categories -> route recommendation

Single Bucharest pilot: default to cash & carry (no MOQ, immediate, zero inventory risk); step up to a national distributor only where cold-chain or quality demands it. Direct import is NOT viable at 1 store - see section 4.

| Category | SKUs (est) | Route (pilot) | Justification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Citrus & tropical (lamai, portocale, banane, mango, ananas, avocado, kiwi) | 7-9 | Cash & carry (Metro/Selgros) | Daily-fresh, low volume, high spoilage on owned stock. Buy-to-shelf same day, no MOQ. Direct citrus import only at multi-store scale. |
| Fish & seafood (somon, ton, creveti, ocean fish) | 4-6 | Distributor - Macromex | Cold-chain critical. Macromex = RO frozen leader (Edenia), reliable cold logistics + e-Factura. C&C backup for a fresh counter. Do NOT improvise cold chain. |
| Coffee, cocoa, tea non-RO (cafea, ciocolata, cacao, ceaiuri) | 6-8 | Distributor - Interbrands/Orbico or Aquila; C&C bridge | Shelf-stable, branded, turnkey listing. Consistent supply + trade terms once volume justifies an account; Metro until then. |
| Olive oil, olives, pasta, Mediterranean | 5-7 | Distributor - Parmafood; C&C bridge | Parmafood specializes in Italian/Mediterranean deli + olive oil + olives. Quality matters on a premium shelf; Metro for launch volume. |
| Exotic dry goods (orez basmati, condimente, nuci: caju/migdale/fistic) | 5-8 | Cash & carry, then TGIE | Shelf-stable, low velocity -> start C&C (no MOQ). Move to TGIE/Aquila once a SKU proves rotation. |
| Non-food / household (detergenti, hartie, cosmetice, hrana animale) | 4-8 selective | Cash & carry or Whiteland/Aquila | Lowest priority for an origin-brand. Stock must-haves only; C&C at launch, distributor if it earns shelf. |
| Out-of-season produce (winter tomatoes/berries) | variable | Gap-on-shelf preferred; C&C if stocked | Default = leave a tasteful "revine in sezon" gap rather than import a watery winter tomato (protects origin credibility). |

## 3. Supply-ecosystem map (RO players)

Full profile table in 08_distributors.csv. Condensed:

Cash & carry (launch backbone):
- Metro - ~30 stores national, full range incl. fresh citrus/tropical, fish counter, coffee, dry, olive oil, non-food. No MOQ, single-unit buy, Metro pro account (~14-30d est, verify), e-Factura. Primary pilot source.
- Selgros - ~20 stores, comparable range. Secondary / price-compare + non-food top-up.
- Trade-off: weakest margin (C&C pro markup then re-mark to retail), but zero MOQ, zero inventory risk, immediate - right for one store.

National distributors (step-up where cold-chain or quality demands):
- Macromex - frozen/chilled leader, fish/seafood (Edenia), full cold chain -> fish/seafood + frozen.
- Parmafood - Italian/Mediterranean deli, olive oil, olives -> Mediterranean specialty.
- Interbrands/Orbico - branded FMCG, coffee/cocoa, household -> coffee, household.
- Aquila - largest RO independent FMCG distributor (BVB-listed), dry FMCG, beverages, coffee, non-food, own fleet -> dry-grocery + non-food breadth.
- Whiteland - broad dry-grocery + non-food -> alternative to Aquila.
- TGIE (Transilvania General Import Export) - Cluj importer+distributor, dry/canned/exotic -> exotic dry goods.
- Trade-off: ~5-15% margin vs direct import (estimate) + listing fees + 30-60d account setup; in return turnkey, reliable, cold-chain handled, e-Factura, no customs exposure.

Direct import (own) - best margin, needs container/pallet volume + customs clearance + ANSVSA import authorization (mandatory for animal-origin: fish) + FX/working capital. Not for 1 store.

## 4. Hybrid sourcing plan + when direct import unlocks

Phase 1 - Pilot (1 store): C&C (Metro primary, Selgros secondary) for citrus/tropical, exotic dry, non-food, out-of-season; Macromex account for fish/seafood (cold chain non-negotiable); Parmafood for olive oil/Mediterranean once volume opens an account. Coffee/cocoa: Metro bridge -> Interbrands/Orbico or Aquila when justified. No direct import.

Phase 2 - ~3-5 stores (single metro): Open distributor accounts (Macromex / Parmafood / Aquila or Interbrands / TGIE) - pooled volume earns listing terms + delivery, retires most C&C dependence (keep C&C for fresh citrus top-up + tail SKUs).

Phase 3 - ~5-8+ stores / pallet-week per category: Evaluate direct import for the two highest-volume best-margin lines - citrus (oranges/lemons/bananas) and coffee. Triggers (all must hold, verify): (1) sustained >= ~1 pallet/week per SKU group; (2) customs broker + ANSVSA import authorization in place (animal-origin fish); (3) working capital to pre-finance + carry FX and transit inventory. Until all three hold, distributor margin (~5-15% est) is cheaper than the fixed cost + risk of self-import. Direct import is an efficiency play at scale, not a launch tactic.

## 5. Cost / margin impact (estimate - verify with vendor quotes)

- Cash & carry route: weakest margin. COGS = C&C pro price; HAMBARUL re-mark may yield only ~10-20% GM on import SKUs (estimate). Acceptable: import ~10-15% of shelf, a basket-completion service not a profit center.
- Distributor route: ~5-15% distributor margin baked into cost; HAMBARUL can target ~20-30% GM (estimate) with negotiated listing terms.
- Direct import (Phase 3 only): best landed cost, but add customs + freight + ANSVSA + spoilage/FX; net advantage only at volume.
- Blended impact: import long-tail at ~10-15% of SKUs and lower GM slightly dilutes the ~36% blended catalog GM. Keep import shelf small; lean on high-margin RO core (honey +45%, preserves +42%, traditional +45%) to carry blended margin.

---

## 6. Strategic risk note

National distributors (Aquila, Macromex, Whiteland, etc.) are also offtake competitors for HAMBARUL Romanian producers - they offer guaranteed volume offtake and can out-bid a small chain for producer attention/supply. Mitigation: keep direct producer contracts (with CUIs) for the RO core; use distributors only on the import long-tail, where there is no producer-relationship conflict. Never let a distributor become the gatekeeper to a hero Romanian SKU.

## 7. Handoffs

- -> logistics-supply: fish/seafood (Macromex) + fresh citrus (C&C) need cold chain; import dry goods tolerate central warehousing. Pilot = mostly C&C pickup/delivery, minimal owned inventory.
- -> business-plan: import share ~10-15% of SKUs / ~10-15% shelf; lower GM (~10-30% est) vs ~36% RO blended - model as basket-completion, slight margin dilution, not growth. Direct import = Phase 3 capex/working-capital item, not Year 1.
- -> brand-marketing: "Din lume" shelf must be visually + verbally separate from "din Romania" - honesty is the brand. No false romanesc labelling.
