# Import & Distribution

Cover the assortment a Romanian-producer-first chain can't source locally — without breaking the producer-first promise.

## Producer-first stays dominant
Imports fill genuine gaps only (citrus, bananas, exotic fruit, coffee, cocoa, some fish/seafood, spices, non-food/household). Quantify local-vs-import split per category; label honestly on shelf (no false "produs românesc").

## Three routes — choose per category, justify
1. **Direct import** — own import (citrus/coffee): best margin, needs volume + customs + ANSVSA import authorization.
2. **National distributor** — turnkey, reliable, costs ~5–15% margin:
   - Aquila, Macromex (frozen/chilled), Interbrands/Orbico, Whiteland, Parmafood (deli/cheese), Transilvania General Import Export.
3. **Cash & carry** — Metro, Selgros: flexible low-volume top-up, weakest margin; good launch bridge.

## Profile each player
category coverage · national/regional · MOQ · payment terms · cold chain · e-Factura readiness. Note: distributors also compete for producers' offtake — a strategic risk.
