# Brand & Marketing

Build the brand and the demand engine around "Romanian producers first".

## The story = traceability + local pride
Every SKU has a named producer (face, farm, county). Surfacing the producer is the moat competitors can't copy. Hambarul = "the barn/granary" — authentic, rustic-modern, trustworthy.

## Deliverables
- **Brand identity:** name rationale, tone, visual cues, tagline (RO + EN).
- **Channels:** local SEO (city/category/producer pages — reuse FastAPI SEO generator pattern), social (FB/Insta/TikTok producer stories), in-store experience, loyalty card, farmers-market events, "supporting Romanian farmers" PR.
- **Go-to-market:** per-store launch campaign, budget, CAC target, expected footfall, KPIs.
