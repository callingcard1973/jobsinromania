# BUSINESS PLAN — HAMBARUL ROMANESC

**Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 2026-06-24 · Synthesis of _workspace artifacts 00–08
**Concept:** Romania first national grocery chain stocked 100% by Romanian producers, with full per-product traceability (producer name, farm, county, CUI/QR code).
**Pilot:** one ~300 m2 neighborhood store, Bucharest, Sector 1 (Floreasca / Dorobanti / Aviatiei).

> Source honesty: hard figures are attributed to INS, Euromonitor, NielsenIQ, GfK and Romanian retail trade press. Where no audited value exists, the number is marked (estimate) and shown as a range. The financial model is a scenario model, not a precise forecast. ANSVSA/ANAF order numbers require legal verification before reliance.

---

## 1. Executive summary

Hambarul Romanesc occupies an empty market space: no national chain today is a pure Romanian-producer retailer with per-product traceability. Every major player (Lidl, Kaufland, Penny, Carrefour, Auchan, Mega Image, Profi) runs produs romanesc as a marketing layer — a shelf, a label, a campaign — on top of an assortment that remains largely private-label and import. Credible regional retailers (Annabella, Diana, Unicarm) have genuine origin but are sub-national, capital-light and brand-weak.

**The defensible idea (the moat):** the whole store is the producer. Every shelf edge and every private-label jar carries a Producer Tag (photo, farm, county, CUI/QR). Incumbents cannot copy this without exposing their import mix and breaking their private-label economics.

**Market:** RO grocery TAM ~EUR 40–45 bn/year (estimate); SAM (modern trade, 5 metros) ~EUR 6–8 bn (estimate); realistic SOM: EUR 4–9 M Year 1 -> EUR 25–45 M Year 3 (estimate).

**Key model figures (estimate):** ~EUR 2.7–3.1 M turnover per store/year, blended gross margin ~34–36%, ~500–600 transactions/day, average basket ~EUR 21–23. Pilot capex ~EUR 215k. Year-1 funding need: ~EUR 500k.

**Critical launch gate:** 100% verified producer CUIs before opening — traceability IS the brand. Plus legal blockers: DSVSA store authorization and an ANAF-connected fiscal cash register.

## 2. Company and vision (Romanian-producer-first)

**Vision:** the chain where you buy Romanian and pay the farmer, not the importer. Every product has a name and a village.

**Mission:** to bring to high-income cities a proximity assortment where Romanian origin is not a shelf but the promise of the entire store — verifiable, visible, traceable.

**The name** — Hambarul is the barn/granary where a household harvest is stored honestly and shared; Romanesc makes the promise non-negotiable: 100% Romanian producers. The name is a contract, not a slogan.

**Decision discipline (project rule):** every major recommendation answers — problem solved? who benefits? revenue impact? cost? — and is ranked by ROI.

## 3. Market study

| Level | Value (estimate) | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| TAM (RO grocery, all channels) | EUR 40–45 bn/year | INS + Euromonitor, triangulated |
| SAM (modern trade, 5 metros) | EUR 6–8 bn/year | ~68% of TAM x metro concentration |
| Addressable origin-premium niche | EUR 200–480 M/year | 3–6% of SAM willing to pay an origin premium |
| SOM Year 1 | EUR 4–9 M | 2–4 pilot stores, ramp |
| SOM Year 3 | EUR 25–45 M | 8–15 stores, 2–3 metros |

**Structure:** modern trade ~65–70% of grocery spend; traditional trade still 30–35%. Discounters (Lidl, Penny) + Kaufland dominate volume; proximity (Mega Image, Profi) dominates store count.

**Tailwinds:** (1) rising origin demand — but self-reported intent overstates behavior, so counted conservatively; (2) short supply chains / farm-to-shelf pushed by CAP; (3) BIO/organic concentrated in the target metros; (4) e-grocery as a viable secondary channel; (5) the premium must be justified by quality + traceability + story, not vanity.

| Lidl / Penny | Discount | Top revenue | produs romanesc as marketing; private-label/import mix |
| Kaufland / Carrefour / Auchan | Hyper | One-stop, large local volumes | Origin is a corner, not the store |

**The white space:** no national chain exposes per-product traceability as its entire promise. **Price benchmark:** premium-origin, compared to the Mega Image/Carrefour premium shelf — NOT to Lidl/Penny discount.

**Premium discipline:** the highest premiums sit on shelf-stable, story-rich categories (honey, wine/tuica, traditional, preserves); fresh categories stay close to mainstream to remain credible on the weekly basket.

**Supplier base:** 15,500 producers (DSVSA/ANSVSA-registered production units), concentrated in Moldova/Transylvania. 36 HAMBARUL private-label SKUs (honey, zacusca, plum butter, flour) — the full margin stays with the brand, and every jar carries a CUI.

**Hero story SKUs:** Salam de Sibiu, acacia honey, Magiun de Topoloveni, pastrami, sheep telemea, Tuica de Bistrita.

**Sourcing gaps (urgent):** wine/tuica (only 5 suppliers), preserves (39); weak email/CUI coverage on dairy/honey/bakery/eggs. Traceability is the brand: placeholder CUIs MUST be resolved to real CUIs before launch.

**Din lume import long-tail (10–15% of the shelf):** citrus/tropical, coffee/cocoa/tea, fish/seafood, exotic dry goods — basket-completion gap-fillers, physically and verbally separated, never labelled Romanian.

## 6. Operations, logistics and ERP

**Model — HYBRID:** metro-local DSD for daily-fresh (bakery, produce, eggs, Bucharest dairy); consolidated chilled 3PL groupage for distant clusters (Suceava, Brasov, Botosani — dairy + meat); shelf-stable lines delivered infrequently; import via cash and carry (Metro/Selgros) + Macromex for fish (zero owned inventory).

**Anti-fragmentation:** one anchor producer per cluster as a collection point; minimum order/frequency terms; one consolidated chilled run per week (twice for dairy).

**Evolution:** at store #2 (Sector 4) — a shared cross-dock hub (~150–250 m2) serving both Bucharest stores; the fixed cost amortizes over 2–3 stores.

**Cold chain:** 70 chilled SKUs + 15 daily-fresh bakery drive the design. Zones: dairy/deli 0–4°C, separate meat 0–4°C, import fish below −18°C (Macromex), produce 8–12°C, eggs ambient, bakery ambient. Rule: unbroken cold chain + data-logger per consignment; reject on breach. Dairy and meat never share a compartment with produce.

**ERP/POS stack — LEAN, RO-SME:** retail POS with native RO fiscalization + inventory (SmartCash/Magister/NextUp) + SME accounting with e-Factura/SAF-T D406 (SmartBill or Saga). SAP B1/Dynamics/Senior ERP deferred until 3 stores + an owned hub. POS enforces batch+expiry per producer CUI. FEFO mandatory.

**Costs (estimate):** Phase 1 logistics+systems opex ~EUR 2,000–4,500/month/store; one-off capex ~EUR 26k–47k (cold chain + POS) per store.

## 7. Locations and rollout (Bucharest-first)

**Pilot format:** 200–400 m2 neighborhood store (target ~300 m2) + loading dock — NOT a big-box. Premium-origin = lower footfall, higher basket.

**Sequence (Bucharest-first):**
1. Store 1 — Bucharest Sector 1 (Floreasca/Dorobanti/Aviatiei): highest metro spend, catchment ~225k, solid supplier base (817). Proves premium-metro economics.
2. Store 2 — Bucharest Sector 4 (Tineretului): lower rent than S1, dense catchment, shared logistics hub.
3. Store 3 — Bucharest Sector 2: backfill, density.
4-5. National expansion: Brasov (best supplier proximity — 919), Iasi (Moldova cluster, cheap rent), Timisoara (west anchor). Cluj deferred — high income but supplier-poor in-county (93) and expensive rent; served later from a Transylvania hub (Brasov/Alba).

> Note: the location study ranked Brasov #1 on supplier proximity; the project constraint nonetheless mandates Bucharest-first, so the pilot stays Sector 1 and the financial model uses Bucharest rent/catchment.

## 8. Marketing and brand

**Identity:** authentic, rustic-modern, trustworthy, proud-not-nationalist. Always name the human and the place (honey from the Pop family, apiary in Bistrita-Nasaud). Palette: granary ochre + warm cream + forest green + barn-wood brown. Avoid the tricolor cliche.

**Tagline:** From their barn, to your table. · Every product has a name and a village. · Buy Romanian. You pay the farmer, not the importer.

**Visual moat — the Producer Tag:** every shelf edge + every private-label pack carries a card with the producer photo, farm, county and CUI/QR. QR to a producer page. Producer of the Month on a front-of-store wall.

**Channels:** (A) Local SEO — reuse the FastAPI page generator (InterJob/AgroEvolution) repointed at the catalog: city x category x producer-CUI pages = thousands of long-tail queries. (B) Social — documentary producer reels (RO+EN social generator). (C) In-store — tastings on hero SKUs, the din lume shelf physically separated. (D) Cardul Hambarul loyalty — points + bonus on the producer-of-the-month SKU; first-party CRM (Brevo/Gmail). (E) Monthly Piata Hambarul market. (F) PR — the only store where you pay the farmer, not the importer.

**Pilot launch budget (one store, 90 days, estimate):** ~EUR 37,000 (SEO 2k, social 5k, paid geo Sector 1 8k, in-store/tags 6k, loyalty 4k, event 5k, PR 3k, contingency 4k). Steady-state ~3–4% of store revenue.

**Targets (estimate):** ~400–700 transactions/day, basket EUR 18–25, CAC EUR 4–7/member (recovered in 1–2 baskets), 3,000–5,000 members in 90 days, repeat rate above 40%/4 weeks. Gate KPI: 100% of SKUs with a verified CUI before opening.

## 9. Compliance

**Two hard blockers (go/no-go):**
1. DSVSA Bucharest sanitary-veterinary registration/authorization (registration for pre-packed vs authorization for portioning/deli — verify the regime).
2. ANAF-connected electronic fiscal cash register (AMEF).

**Retailer obligations (Reg. EC 852/2004, 853/2004, 178/2002, EU 1169/2011):** HACCP plan (CCP = cold chain), temperature monitoring with logs, one-step-back/one-step-forward traceability, withdrawal/RASFF procedure, ABP contract, pest control + potable water, allergen info on loose sales.

**Fiscal (ANAF):** e-Factura (B2B + B2C extension), SAF-T D406, e-VAT, food retail CAEN + VAT — confirm with the accountant.

**Suppliers:** animal-origin producers (dairy, meat, eggs, honey) must hold DSVSA registration/authorization; the store collects proof before listing. Raw milk / raw-milk cheese = highest risk, special rules. BIO/ECO: no bio/organic wording without a valid certificate (Reg. EU 2018/848) — the master data contains no certificates.

> All RO order numbers and EU articles require verification with ANSVSA/DSVSA Bucharest, ANAF and a Romanian lawyer/accountant. Not legal advice.

## 11. Funding ask

**Year-1 need: ~EUR 500,000** = capex ~EUR 215k + working capital ~EUR 150k (stock + supplier terms + cash-and-carry) + ramp/contingency buffer ~EUR 135k.

**Use of funds:** open the Sector 1 pilot + store #2 Sector 4, resolve producer CUIs (the traceability gate), cold-chain fit-out, RO-SME POS/ERP stack, the EUR 37k launch budget and DSVSA/HACCP compliance.

**ROI / ranking:** priority 1 = Sector 1 pilot (highest revenue/store); priority 2 = store #2 + shared hub (amortizes logistics over 2–3 stores); priority 3 = national expansion after repeatability is proven. Distributor accounts and direct import = only at 3–5 and 5–8 stores respectively, not in Year 1.

## 12. Risks and mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| CUI/traceability gate — unresolved placeholder CUIs | Blocks the brand promise itself | 100% verified CUI = launch gate; ANAF/Google Places enrichment; no product on shelf without a CUI |
| Legal blockers DSVSA + cash register | Store cannot open | File early; HACCP consultant; budget + lead time in plan |
| Distant-producer cold chain | #1 food-safety + brand risk | Data-logger per consignment; reject protocol; anchor-producer consolidation |
| Thin margin / perishable waste | Erodes the thin EBITDA | Mandatory FEFO, low stock, daily ordering; waste KPI by category; capital buffer |
| Distributors as offtake competitors | Can outbid a small chain for producer supply | Direct contracts (with CUIs) for the RO core; distributors only on the import long-tail |
| Origin intent overstated (self-reported) | Footfall below expectation | Counted conservatively; tastings + loyalty as real conversion levers |
| Supplier fragmentation (15,500, tiny drops) | Logistics cost, buyer time | Hybrid: local DSD + consolidated groupage; min order/frequency |
| Category gaps (wine/tuica 5, preserves 39) | Thin assortment | Urgent sourcing of 15–20 distillers/wineries + 30+ preserve workshops |
| Bucharest rent / catchment (field estimates) | Model error | Field-verify before lease signing; small format de-risks |

## 13. Roadmap

- Phase 0 (pre-launch, 0–3 months): resolve CUIs (the gate), DSVSA authorization + cash register, contract core + hero suppliers, POS/ERP stack, cold-chain fit-out, build SEO + Producer Tags.
- Phase 1 (Year 1): launch Sector 1 pilot (event + Piata Hambarul + PR); open store #2 Sector 4.
- Phase 2 (Year 2): store #3 Sector 2 + shared Bucharest cross-dock hub; open distributor accounts for import; first expansion stores (Brasov/Iasi).
- Phase 3 (Year 3): scale to ~7–8 stores across 2–3 metros; evaluate direct import (citrus, coffee); Transylvania regional hub (Brasov/Alba) to unlock Cluj.

**Strategic optionality:** Annabella / Diana / Unicarm = benchmark + potential partnerships/acquisitions for faster nationalization.
